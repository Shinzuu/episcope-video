Two detectors on seasonally adjusted counts

EARS C2: this week against weeks 3 to 9 back. Alarm when z > 3.

z = (y − s·m) / σ

CUSUM: S = max(0, S + z − 0.5), alarm when S > 5. It adds up small, steady excess.

Farrington needs about five years before its first test week. That is all the history we have.

Real output: weekly dengue in Dhaka, triangles are CUSUM alerts (draft run).

Privacy by design, and an equity risk

  • Keyed hash for patient IDs; age in bands.
  • Counts 1 to 4 hidden, with complementary suppression so totals cannot reveal them.
  • k-anonymity check on published tables.
  • Weak reporting looks like good health. The equity floor is one guard against that.

Hatched cells: suppressed counts under 5.

What we would tell the Ministry

  1. Dengue supplies in place by week 29, before the season.
  2. Treat alerts as a short list; check reporting completeness before acting.
  3. Choose α openly, knowing what it costs.

Limits

  • Synthetic data: no real decision yet.
  • Streaming alerts: status pending.
